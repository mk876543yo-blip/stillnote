Stillnote is a calm, local-first study space for notes and active recall.

• Write notes with autosave, collections, tags, search, Markdown preview, templates, and a trash folder.
• Dictate notes with Android speech recognition or browser speech input.
• Make flashcards from definitions and key sentences in your notes, then review them with a spaced schedule.
• Use a focus timer and study insights to keep a steady rhythm.
• Export and import a backup whenever you need it.
Notes, cards, preferences, and study history are stored locally on your device. Stillnote does not require an account, cloud sync, or network access. Dictation uses the speech recognition service installed on your device; its availability and network behavior depend on that service.
